How it works

Simple steps to better care — and a reward

SaveWell makes it easy to find the right care for your upcoming procedure. Here's what to expect.

Why it matters

The same procedure — for a lot less

Many common procedures — like colonoscopies, cataract surgeries, and joint replacements — can be performed at an outpatient surgical center (ASC) instead of a hospital. Both settings deliver high-quality care, but outpatient centers cost Medicare significantly less.

The difference in cost is what SaveWell passes back to you. When you choose a SaveWell top-rated specialist and outpatient center for your procedure, it saves the healthcare system money, which we pass on to you as a reward.

The process

How SaveWell works

When you need a procedure, a SaveWell care guide is here to help you find the right care — and make the most of your benefits.

1

Reach out to SaveWell

Call us or submit a contact form. A SaveWell care guide will connect with you to learn more about your upcoming procedure, location, and coverage — so we can start finding the right match.

2

We identify your best options

Your care guide researches trusted, accredited outpatient surgical centers and board-certified specialists in your area that match your specific procedure — and explains your options in plain language.

3

Choose the right care for you

The choice is always yours. You're never obligated to use any particular provider. Your care guide is simply here to make the decision easier and ensure you feel confident about your care.

4

Complete your procedure and earn your reward

If you have a qualifying Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan and complete your procedure at a recommended outpatient center, a credit is automatically applied to your Medicare Supplement premium at the end of the year — no claim to file, no hoops to jump through.

Our standards

How we select our providers

Quality matters. We carefully select every provider in the SaveWell network based on four criteria.

01

Clinical quality measures

Evidence-based standards of care — we look at whether providers follow established best practices for your specific procedure.

02

Patient outcomes

A track record of successful treatment — we evaluate results, complication rates, and readmission data where available.

03

Patient satisfaction

Positive reviews and feedback from patients — because how you're treated matters just as much as clinical outcomes.

04

Board certification

Current credentials and specialization — every physician in our network is board-certified and focused on their area of expertise.
